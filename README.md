# Excel Sales Performance Analysis and Marketing Strategy

This portfolio project explores sales performance, customer efficiency, and marketing ROI optimization — developed entirely in Excel as part of an advanced data analytics and marketing strategy course.

The analysis is based on a simulated dataset for *TechLife*, a consumer electronics company aiming to identify the key factors driving sales performance and optimize marketing investments through data-driven insights.

---

## Project Goals

- Analyze customer segments by age, region, and spending tiers  
- Identify seasonal sales patterns and performance peaks  
- Assess marketing efficiency and ROI across customer segments  
- Develop integrated strategic recommendations to maximize profitability  

---

## Key Techniques

- Descriptive statistics and KPI dashboards (revenue, cost, ROI)
- Pivot tables for segmentation (age, region, spending)
- Correlation and regression (marketing cost vs sales)
- Cost-to-revenue ratio and conversion analysis
- Insight writing and integrated strategy design

---

## Executive Summary

This analysis revealed that:
- **Established Adults** and **Mature Customers** drive the highest profitability  
- **Medium and High Spenders** generate the majority of revenue at optimal cost levels  
- **Marketing spend** shows negligible correlation with sales (R² = 0.0094), revealing inefficiencies  
- **Seasonal peaks** occur in **spring (Apr–May)** and **autumn (Oct–Nov)**  
- **Aosta Valley** and **Calabria** outperform other regions, while northern areas show growth potential  

The final strategy recommends reallocating marketing budgets toward high-value segments, optimizing timing and personalization, and improving ROI through smarter, data-driven targeting and budgeting optimisation.
